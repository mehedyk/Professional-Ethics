Deontology — অলঙ্ঘনীয় নিয়ম

ফোকাস: নিয়ম ও কাজ, ফলাফল না।
ধারণা: খারাপ ফল হলেও কাজটা ঠিক, যদি তা কর্তব্য মেনে হয়।

Kant-এর Categorical Imperative

কাজটা তখনই ঠিক, যখন তুমি চাইবে সেটা সবার জন্য সার্বজনীন আইন হোক।

অনুমতি ছাড়া data কখনো নয় — জীবন-রক্ষাকারী মডেলের জন্যও না। ফল যা-ই হোক, নিয়ম ভাঙা যাবে না।

Computer Ethics-এর ৪ ভিত্তি

১. Respect for Privacy

সম্মতি ছাড়া data সংগ্রহ বা শেয়ার নয়। Data নিরাপদে রাখো।

২. Honesty & Integrity

Plagiarism নয়। Backdoor বা malicious code নয়।

৩. Responsibility

নিজের software-এর দায় নাও। ক্ষতি হলেও flaw ও security risk report করো।

৪. Fairness & Non-discrimination

Algorithmic bias এড়াও। Race বা gender-এর ভিত্তিতে বৈষম্য নয়।

Answer 1 · 4 marks

Q1. Identify two ethical issues

2

1Violation of privacy & lack of informed consent

Students' names, email addresses, academic records and login activity are used for testing without informing them or obtaining consent. This violates their right to privacy and data-protection principles and laws (ICT Act 2006, Digital Security Act 2018).

2

2Concealment of wrongdoing & conflict of duties

The project manager asks Nusrat to stay silent to protect the release date, putting deadlines above honesty, integrity and accountability. Her loyalty to the employer conflicts with her duty to the students whose data is misused. Silence would make her complicit.

Tip: Each issue = name it + use words from the scenario + explain why it is wrong.
Answer 2 · 7 marks · part 1

Q2(a). Deontological analysis

Framework

Deontology judges an action by whether it follows moral duties and rules, regardless of the outcome.

Kant's Categorical Imperative

If everyone used personal data without consent, trust in digital systems would collapse — so the rule cannot be universalised.

Nusrat's duties

To respect students' privacy, to be honest, and to take responsibility for the system she helps build.

Manager's arguments fail

"Internal only" and "benefits thousands" are outcome-based; they cannot override a duty. The rule is not broken because the result is good.

Verdict: Nusrat must report the misuse, even if the release is delayed.
Answer 2 · 7 marks · part 2

Q2(b). Utilitarian analysis

Which action gives the greatest good for the greatest number? Weigh benefits against harms.

Staying silent

Benefit: on-time launch; students benefit sooner; no delay cost.

Harm: real data in testing → insider misuse & breach risk; legal penalties; lost trust & reputation; leaked data is permanent.

Reporting & fixing

Cost: a temporary delay.

Fix: use anonymised / dummy data or obtain consent. Protects the very students the platform serves; builds long-term trust.

Verdict: harms of silence are larger, longer-lasting and hit the same users ➜ net benefit favours reporting.

Answer 3 · 4 marks

Q3. One ICT-ethics principle

Principle: Respect for Privacy (with informed consent)

Computer ethics and the ACM Code ("respect privacy") require professionals not to collect, use or share user data without consent, and to keep it secure.

Why it applies

Names, emails, academic records and login activity are used without the students' knowledge or permission.

Justification

Asymmetric power: students can't see how data is handled — they depend on engineers. Backed by ICT Act 2006 & DSA 2018.

Conclusion: Nusrat is obliged to act; silence for a deadline would also break the principle of responsibility — report flaws and risks even when it is costly.
